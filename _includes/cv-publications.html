It is built without depending on the stability of Liquid's `sort`, which Ruby does not guarantee: `map: "year" | sort | reverse | uniq` is safe because the year values are distinct, and each year group is then pulled out with `where` (which preserves the order of _data/publications.yml) and reversed.
